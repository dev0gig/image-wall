Visual Archive Guide

Supported Sources

This tool supports Bluesky and Mastodon accounts, Reddit subreddits and X (Twitter) accounts. For Bluesky, type the handle (e.g. esa.int, someone.bsky.social) or paste the profile URL. For Mastodon, type the full address (e.g. @eff@mastodon.social) or paste the profile URL. For Reddit, type r/EarthPorn or paste the subreddit URL. For X, type the handle (e.g. ArchDigest), use an @ prefix, or paste the profile URL.

Limits

Bluesky and Mastodon deliver up to 100 images per account — both are queried directly by your browser, no relay in between. Reddit delivers up to 100 images per subreddit through its own relay (see worker/); if that relay is unavailable, the app falls back to the free feed service and 10 images. X is capped at 20 images per account.

Favorites & Downloads

Click the heart icon on any image to save it to your Favorites. In the Favorites view, you can download all your curated images as a single ZIP archive. Reddit and Bluesky images are routed through the image relay images.weserv.nl for this, because their own servers refuse downloads made by JavaScript. Mastodon servers allow it directly.

Import & Export

Use the Backup options to export your saved channel list to a JSON file. You can import this file later or on another device to restore your channel collection. Note: This only backs up the channel handles, not the images themselves.
